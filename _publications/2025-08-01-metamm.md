### Project Overview
Designing a multimodal meta-learning framework to enable rapid adaptation across vision, language, and audio tasks. 

### Key Contributions
* **Cross-Modal Transfer:** Developing cross-modal transfer mechanisms using shared latent representations and meta-learned optimization strategies.
* **Rigorous Evaluation:** Evaluating performance on benchmark datasets to measure adaptation speed, generalization, and robustness across modalities.
